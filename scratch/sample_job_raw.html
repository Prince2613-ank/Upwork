Video Editor for Documentary-Style YouTube Channel (Ongoing Work, 3–5 Videos/Week)

Posted yesterday

Worldwide

Summary

I run a Youtube channel that breaks down real criminal cases using police interrogation footage. I'm looking for an experienced editor to cut each episode in a documentary style, including a highly edited intro similar to channels like Bloodlines and Red Thread Interrogation. Reference videos (the style I'm looking for): - https://youtu.be/vcYOWAIVPgA - https://youtu.be/5ffPHzxNB_o - https://youtu.be/bgXTSfDpdMM What I provide for each episode: - The interrogation footage - Commentary and voiceover recordings - Basic case details What you'll do: - Asset research: Find the aerial flyovers, mugshots, surveillance stills, news article screenshots and court documents for each case. Using Claude or ChatGPT makes this a quick process. Please double-check that every photo shows the right person, since many names are shared by unrelated people. - Recap/intro (1–2 min): Case recap with date/time titles, location tags, polaroid photo drops, article highlight animations, a countdown timer, CCTV effects, color grading and sound design - Footage section: Interrogation footage with commentary, subtitles and clean pacing - Outro: Short closing segment Pay: - Recap/intro and outro: $40 flat per episode - Main section: $1 per minute of final edited runtime (for example, 20 minutes = $20, 30 minutes = $30) Requirements: - Proven experience with documentary-style or true crime edits. Please include links to similar work. Applications without relevant samples won't be considered. - Strong motion graphics skills - Ability to build a reusable template so episodes stay consistent - Capacity to handle 3–5 videos per week - Good communication and reliable turnaround This is ongoing work for the right editor. To apply: Share 2–3 links to documentary-style edits you've done.
